# Managed Deployment

Ocular will always be available as a self-hosted solution, but if you don't want to worry about hosting and maintaining your own instance, there are also managed deployment options available.

However, little of the revenue is usually passed down to the actual developer.
So please consider [supporting me](https://github.com/sponsors/simonwep) directly if you like it ❤️

## PikaPods

> [!TIP]
> I get a 20% cut of the revenue generated by Ocular on PikaPods.️
> This helps me to continue to develop and maintain Ocular :)

Ocular is available as a managed deployment on [PikaPods](https://www.pikapods.com/pods?run=ocular), a platform for hosting and managing applications in the cloud.
Click [here](https://www.pikapods.com/pods?run=ocular) to deploy Ocular on PikaPods.

## Cloudzy

> [!WARNING]
> Cloudzy is a legitimate VPS/cloud hosting provider, but prospective users should be aware of some reputational and operational concerns.
> In 2023, security firm [Halcyon published research](https://www.halcyon.ai/blog/report-ransomware-command-and-control-providers-unmasked-by-halcyon-researchers) alleging that Cloudzy infrastructure was being used by cybercrime and state-sponsored threat actors; [Cloudzy disputed these allegations](https://cloudzy.com/news/official-statement-august-2023/) and states that it actively terminates abuse and cooperates with law enforcement.
> Additionally, [Trustpilot currently states](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/cloudzy.com) that Cloudzy's rating is unavailable due to a breach of its guidelines and that a number of fake reviews were removed.

Ocular is available as a managed deployment on [Cloudzy](https://cloudzy.com/marketplace/ocular), a platform for hosting and managing applications in the cloud.
Click [here](https://cloudzy.com/marketplace/ocular) to deploy Ocular on Cloudzy.
